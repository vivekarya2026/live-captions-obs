Live Captions

Live captions and translation, English ↔ Hindi / Hinglish, with the Nirankari vocabulary. Works with OBS (captions on your stream), without OBS, and can write into a Google Doc.

  • Installs Live Captions in Applications.
  • If OBS is installed: adds the Live Captions dock and caption overlay to OBS (and the LocalVocal plugin). Close OBS first, or it is done the next time you start Live Captions with OBS closed.
  • After installing, the Live Captions window opens and downloads the speech and translation models once (about 4 GB). Everything then runs on this Mac; audio never leaves it.
  • macOS will ask once to allow the microphone.

Requires macOS 12 or later. This installer is for Macs with @CHIP@.
